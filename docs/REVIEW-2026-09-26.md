## 2. What today changed

**(a) The instrument got stronger — and this is the first real capability gain in weeks.**
`scripts/oof_surrogate.py` assembles every component that *is* fold-trained (A5's per-fold
checkpoints, which the stage's own loader asserts by filename; plus the four public20 seed-arms),
rank-blends them with weights **chosen on four folds and applied to the fifth**, and reaches
**0.9151 on 4406 studies** against A5's 0.9089 alone (+0.0062, masked paired CI [+0.0048, +0.0076]).
The self-test (pairing a prediction with itself → delta 0.0000, CI [0.0000, 0.0000]) passes.
That is **+0.033 stronger and 5× wider** than the stand-in every previous probe used. It is
deliberately **Rad-free**, so the calibrator's `reference`/`second` inputs stay independent of it.

**(b) The `rad_cal_w` question got measured properly — and then the measurement was falsified.**
This is the important part of the review, including the retraction.

| what was measured, on n=4406 | gated-label macro |
|---|---|
| `rad_cal_w = 0.00` | 0.8972 |
| `rad_cal_w = 0.40` (shipped) | 0.9048 |
| best dose, **chosen on four folds and applied to the fifth** | **0.9067** (w = 0.7–0.8 in *all five* folds) |

That is `+0.0075` for the calibrator over off, and `+0.0020` more from moving the dose 0.40 → 0.8,
with a stable per-fold choice — the signature of a real effect rather than a lottery. It reproduces
A117's direction at five times the n and on a stronger base, and it looked like the first
score-relevant lever with proper local support in a long time. **It is not.**

**(c) Why it is not: the rival explanation, and the test that kills it.**
The calibrator's input vector contains **both** `base_rank` and `reference_rank` — i.e. both sides of
the blend it is applied to. So on any blend whose weights are mis-set, the calibrator can *repair*
the blend, and a probe that mis-sets them will credit the calibrator with the repair. Sweeping
`rad_alpha` (the reference weight) against `rad_cal_w`, gated labels, n=4406:

| rad_alpha | cal_w=0.00 | cal_w=0.40 | cal_w=0.80 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.00** | **0.9185** | 0.9179 | 0.9114 |
| 0.10 | **0.9185** | 0.9169 | 0.9107 |
| 0.20 | **0.9169** | 0.9153 | 0.9100 |
| 0.30 | **0.9137** | 0.9131 | 0.9091 |
| 0.40 | 0.9086 | **0.9103** | 0.9082 |
| 0.50 | 0.9015 | 0.9068 | 0.9073 |
| 0.55 *(shipped)* | 0.8972 | 0.9048 | **0.9067** |
| 0.70 | 0.8819 | 0.8977 | **0.9051** |
| 1.00 (pure reference) | 0.8427 | 0.8796 | **0.9014** |

Two things are visible at once:

1. **The global optimum of the surface is `rad_alpha ≈ 0, cal_w = 0`** (0.9185) — i.e. **with the
   blend well-set, the calibrator is worth about nothing** (−0.0006), and turning it *up* costs.
2. **The calibrator's optimal dose rises with `rad_alpha`** (0 → 0.4 → 0.8 as alpha goes 0 → 0.55 →
   1.0). A correction term behaves exactly like this; an information source does not.

⇒ **The `+0.0075` and the dose-0.8 finding are, at least predominantly, the calibrator undoing a
mis-weighted Rad reference in our surrogate.** ⇒ **Do not build or submit `rad_cal_w = 0.8`.**

**(d) A117's rejection of `0943r` loses its basis.** A117 concluded "the calibrator carries
information, so the frontier's v38 (0.40 → 0.00) points the wrong way — do not spend a slot". The
measurement behind that is the one just shown to be confounded. `0943r` therefore reverts to what
A105 originally said: **a bet with a public source that we cannot verify locally** — not a
locally *contradicted* idea. A117's own caveat ① anticipated a strength problem; it did not
anticipate the overlap problem, which is the one that actually bites.

⚠️ **The generalisable lesson, and it is not specific to the calibrator: a probe that evaluates a
correction term while the thing it corrects is mis-set will attribute the correction to the term.**
Any future "does X add information on top of Y?" test must first show that Y is at its own optimum —
otherwise the test measures repair.

## 4. Ranked open items

Ranked by **measurability**, since that is the binding constraint.

1. **Create the two missing candidate arms so the deadline decision has a real choice set.**
   `0943r` (`rad_cal_w` → 0.00, the frontier's own v38 revision — now *un*-contradicted by §2d) and
   `0943p` (`parent`: flatten the outer routing entirely — the frontier's own registered insurance,
   per the source comment in A122). Both are one-token forks of an already-working notebook, both
   cost one submission slot and no GPU. ⚠️ **This widens the choice set; it does not raise the
   expected score**, because the board cannot rank these arms and we will still pick two.
   *Kill condition:* if either fails preflight or the hidden rerun, drop it.
2. **Monitor the frontier, cheaply and rarely** (§167's method; the last sweep found nothing).
   This is the only path to a *new* score, since it is the only source of an unmodelled idea.
   *Kill condition:* none — it is one `scan_frontier.py` run when there is reason to look.
3. **Instrument-building, if we want to keep resolving knobs.** Today moved the surrogate
   0.882 → 0.915 on 5× the studies. The next step would be a genuine DINO-stage OOF, and **it does
   not exist to be built** — the members are not fold-trained. So this line ends here; treat the
   surrogate as final unless a fold-trained DINO source appears.
4. **Nothing else.** The image/metadata line is measured dead (A112), the training line is measured
   dead (A106), fusion is closed four ways (§98.3), and the five remaining knobs are all gated on
   the same missing instrument.

## 5. The conclusion to carry forward

**The score is at the public ceiling, and the residual uncertainty is selection risk on knobs that
were fitted to data we cannot see — which is unmeasurable by construction, not by lack of effort.**
The rational play for the remaining four weeks is therefore *narrow and boring*:

- hold the ceiling (done — five arms submitted, all `COMPLETE`);
- keep the final choice deferred, and decide at the deadline among arms that hedge **different**
  unmeasurable axes (the argument in `FINAL-SELECTION.md`);
- look at the frontier occasionally;
- and do **not** spend the remaining weeks turning knobs, because §2 is what happens when we do:
  a clean-looking `+0.0020`, stable across five folds, that turns out to be an artefact of the
  instrument. That result cost nothing and it is the most useful thing this review produced.
