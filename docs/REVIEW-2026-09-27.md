# Review, 2026-09-27 — the ceiling was wrong, and that changes the whole plan

Written after all nine arms read back (0.943 ×4, 0.942 ×4, 0.941) and **after reading the live
leaderboard for the first time in this project's recent history**. The one-line finding:

> ⭐⭐ **The public ceiling is 0.959, not 0.943.** Our 0.943 is the *public-notebook plateau* — 448
> teams sit on exactly that number. There are **218 teams measurably above us**, 72 at ≥ 0.950 and 24
> at ≥ 0.955. The gap to the top is **0.016 — twice the ±0.008 noise band**, so it is a real,
> reproducible difference and not a lottery.

> ⭐⭐⭐ **And the number that actually matters is +0.001, not +0.016.** Our row is **rank 411, score
> 0.943**. That score is a **448-team block spanning ranks 219–666**, and **both medal cutoffs fall
> inside it** (silver = top 5% = rank ≤ 222; bronze = top 10% = rank ≤ 444). So at 0.943, *whether we
> medal at all is decided by an opaque tie-break, not by our score.* The next score up — **0.944** — is
> only a **46-team block** at ranks 173–218. ⇒ **+0.001 escapes the tie-break entirely and lands in
> silver.** ⚠️ Cutoffs are the standard Kaggle rule and medals are awarded on the **private** LB, so
> this is indicative rather than decisive — but "the quantity we must move is 0.001–0.003, not 0.016"
> follows regardless.

⚠️ **And this review made the same class of error it is about, one hour after stating the rule.**
`lb_ceiling.py` originally **derived** our rank as "teams strictly above + 1" and printed **219** —
while the file it had *already downloaded* contained a `Rank` column saying **411**, because Kaggle
gives every team a **distinct rank inside a block of equal scores**. The rule ("never carry a cheaply
measurable quantity as a belief") is not satisfied by writing it down; it requires reading the
authoritative field. Both bugs are now covered by `--selftest`.

Everything in [`REVIEW-2026-09-26.md`](REVIEW-2026-09-26.md) about *instruments* still holds and is
reused below. Its **conclusion did not**: it said "the score is at the public ceiling, so the residual
is unmeasurable selection risk". The residual was not unmeasurable. We were reading a stale number.

---

## 1. How the error happened — and it is the most important lesson in this document

The belief "0.941 is the public ceiling" traces to **A87.1**, which read the *text* of public claims
("highest declared scores were all `[0.941 SOTA]`"). That is a claim made by other competitors at some
earlier date, on a discussion thread — **not the leaderboard**. We then built a month of strategy on it:
`FINAL-SELECTION.md`, the whole "hold the ceiling" framing, and the review's conclusion that the
remaining work is unmeasurable-by-construction.

**The live leaderboard is a free, authenticated, one-command instrument that had never been read.**
We were using a secondary source to estimate a primary quantity, while the primary sat one API call
away.

| | |
|---|---|
| what we believed | public ceiling ≈ 0.941–0.943, and we hold it |
| what is true | public max **0.959**; **666 teams at ≥ 0.943**; 24 at ≥ 0.955 |
| cost of the error | ~1 week of strategy aimed at *selection risk* instead of *score* |
| the fix | this document, plus a **standing rule**: the ceiling is re-read from the leaderboard, never from claims |

⚠️ **Generalisable form:** *a quantity that is cheaply and authoritatively measurable must never be
carried as a remembered belief.* This is the same failure family as the rest of the repo's traps
("the gate does not look at the thing it certifies"), applied to a number rather than a gate.

**0.955 on 8 submissions**, and several others did it on 41–49. A team cannot hill-climb to 0.955 in
eight tries. ⇒ **A real recipe exists.**

### 2.3 The public notebooks confirm the plateau

The two most recently-run public notebooks were pulled and read:

* `romantamrazov/rsna-knee-dinosaur-v5` (107 votes, updated 09-26) says in its own words: *"Strong

  0.943 path.
* `manderson240/cohezion-rsna-knee-sota-ensemble` (ran **today**) calls itself an ensemble and its
  only substantive change is **adding one more published CoAtNet reader**.

⇒ **The public ecosystem is stuck at 0.943 and is trying to climb by adding per-finding specialists
and more members.** Nobody in it trains anything. The 0.955+ work is private.

## 3. Where the gap can actually come from

Hypotheses, ranked by (evidence × our ability to test it):

| # | hypothesis | evidence for | evidence against | testable by us? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | **They train their own models**, where the public cluster only blends published ones | the public plateau is exactly "blend everything published"; nobody public trains; 24 teams are above it | our training line was closed (A106) — but **on the wrong criterion**, see §3.1 | ⭐ **yes** |
| **H2** | Per-finding specialists (a dedicated model per finding or finding-group) | `romantamrazov` does exactly this and it is the public cluster's own climb attempt | only 3 of 12 findings, and it is not yet proven to move the board | ⭐ yes, cheaply, on our OOF |
| **H3** | Better labels (LLM-soft labels / distillation / pseudo-labelling) drive better training | a large label ecosystem exists (`llm-labels`, `rsna-knee-labels` = *distilled soft labels*, `psuedo-labeling-knee` 09-24) | we already mount `pilkwang/rsna-knee-llm-labels` | ⭐ yes, but only through training |
| **H4** | Higher resolution / denser slice input at *inference* | A5's edge is 16-slice density (A149); native-384 corpora exist | we already mount native384 and dense checkpoints | partly |
| **H5** | The gap is mostly public hill-climbing and will shrink privately | median 83 submissions above us | `daimaru` 0.955 on 8 submissions | ✗ not until the deadline |

### 3.1 ⭐ H1 needs a correction, and it is the strategic unlock

The training line was closed by **A106**: our best arm 0.8731 vs A5's 0.9143 on 181 common held-out
studies, CI excluding zero. That measured **"our arm cannot beat the frontier's single best arm"** —
which is true, and which is *not the question*.

**For ensemble contribution, the requirement is not to beat A5. It is to be strong *and*
decorrelated.** We already proved that gate works: adding our rescued `cn0` (0.8769 — *below* A5) to a
parent-quality base gave **+0.0028, CI [+0.0011, +0.0043]**, with **both halves independently choosing
weight 0.2 out of `public20`'s share** (A131/A137). A member weaker than A5 added measurable score.

⇒ **The closed line was "beat A5". The open line is "add decorrelated members".** These are different
questions and we conflated them. `cn0` is the existence proof, and it is already shipped in
`0943cf` (0.943).

## 5. The plan

**Objective, restated honestly:** get **above 0.943** with changes that are validated on a
non-public instrument.

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The target is +0.001** | 0.943 is a 448-team tie block that **contains both medal cutoffs**; 0.944 is a 46-team block. **+0.001 is the difference between "medal decided by tie-break" and "silver".** |
| Why that is reachable | a **measured +0.0028** member already exists (`cn0`, shipped in `0943cf`), and it was measured on a **0.917** base — the same size as the increment we need |
| ⚠️ What an increment of that size requires | it must be **real**, not fitted. A +0.003 that only exists on the public split is worth nothing privately; that is why every addition goes through the out-of-sample-weight gate (A127's lesson) |
| ⚠️ Why not "reach 0.959" | part of the 0.016 is public overfitting whose private transfer is unknown, and we cannot see the leaders' methods. Treating 0.959 as the target would optimise for the public split — the exact failure A122 documents in the frontier's own routing weights |

## 7. What does not change

* **Never spend a slot to "see if it helps"** for a 0.001–0.003 effect (A96). ⚠️ This review does not
  relax that: 0.002 is still invisible. What changed is that **0.016 is visible**, so a *large*
  validated change may now be confirmed on the board — that is a different use of the same rule.
* **The five public-fitted knobs stay unjudgeable** (A93 — no parent OOF). `FINAL-SELECTION.md`'s hedge
  logic is unchanged, and `0943cf` + `0943p` remains the pre-registered recommendation.
* **The hidden rerun is the only real test**; every arm must complete it before it is selectable.
* **Report labels are noisy** — the local instrument supports relative comparisons, not absolute ones.

## 8. Honesty limits on this review

* The leaderboard is the **public** split. Teams above us have median 83 submissions, so an unknown
  fraction of the 0.016 is public overfitting. ⚠️ **We cannot currently estimate how much.**
* We can see *that* the leaders do better, not *how*. H1–H4 are hypotheses; only H1 and H2 are
  testable with instruments we own.
* Our local instrument resolves ~0.002 and the gap is 0.016 ⇒ closing it needs either ~8 validated
  additions or one structural change. **A single new member will not close it.**
* The one thing this review is confident about is the **negative**: we are not at the ceiling, we are
  at the public plateau, and the two are 0.016 apart.
