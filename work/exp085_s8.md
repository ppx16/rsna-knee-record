## 8. Second gate run: the slice policy is NOT the gap, and the config says why

`ppxl16/rsna-knee-exp085-slices` v1 ran to **COMPLETE** (exit 0, no failure, ~64 min CPU). Design:
order A only (settled by §2), 4 slice policies x 4 crop scales, with the union of all policies'
slices **embedded once per crop** so every policy costs only a head pass.

**crop 336 / `linspace` = 0.7929, bit-for-bit the first run's number.** That is a genuine

readable: the instrument is stable, so the differences below are real.

### 8.2 ⚠️ All three "band" readings are WORSE, and the crop barely matters

* every band policy loses to plain `linspace` at every crop (0.7553–0.7851 against 0.7861–0.8020);
* the crop scale moves the result by at most **0.016** across 240–336 px, so `crop_mm 130.0` is **not**
  the missing 0.10 either.

⇒ **Two of the three candidate explanations for the 0.7997 → 0.9062 gap are now eliminated**: it is
neither *which* slices are chosen nor *how tightly* they are cropped.

### 8.3 ⭐ What the config points at instead

⚠️ Every experiment so far, mine included, has computed **one** forward pass over all 12 slices and
attended across them. But the checkpoint's config says:

```
n_group 4 | group_pick per_study | eval_group_agg mean      <- "mean" aggregation over GROUPS
slice_attention_queries 1 | target_specific_slice_attention True
```

**`eval_group_agg: mean` is an aggregation over FOUR GROUPS, not over 12 slices.** The natural reading
-- and the one that would explain a systematic ~0.10 shortfall -- is that the model is applied **once
per group of 3 slices**, producing 12 logits per group, and the final prediction is the **mean of the
4 groups' logits**. The depth encoding (`slice_pool.depth_mlp`) may likewise be the **within-group**
depth rather than the global depth, since `group_pick: per_study` picks groups independently.

That is a different computation over the same weights, it is cheap to test with the caching design
already in place, and **it is the only remaining structural difference between our forward and the
author's.**

### 8.4 Next run, concretely

Same harness, `linspace` at the best crop, varying only the aggregation:

1. **pool 12 -> head** (the current control, 0.8020);
2. **4 groups x 3 -> head -> mean of logits** (`eval_group_agg: mean`, global depth);
3. **4 groups x 3 -> head -> mean of logits**, **within-group depth**;
4. as 2/3 but `max` and `median` aggregation as controls.

If (2) or (3) reaches ~0.90, the reconstruction is complete and exp085 clears the A167 member gate;
if all four land at ~0.80, the gap is in the corpus preprocessing itself (`train_window_sampling:
overlap`, the windowing/intensity convention) rather than in anything the config names.
