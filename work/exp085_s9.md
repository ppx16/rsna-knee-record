### 9.2 ⭐ The config's word was right: the groups are aggregated by MEAN

**`grp_mean_*` beats `pool12` at both crops** (+0.016 to +0.025), and — the part that makes it a
*finding* rather than a lucky cell — **`grp_max` and `grp_median` do NOT**: they land at or below the
pooling control (0.7997/0.7841 and 0.7890/0.8002). So the gain is specific to **mean aggregation over
the four groups**, which is exactly what the checkpoint's `eval_group_agg` field says. The model is
evaluated **once per group of 3 slices**, and the four groups' logits are averaged.

That is the second structural piece recovered from the config alone, after the head order (§2):
* **order A** (per-finding classifier): +0.13 over B/C;
* **per-group mean aggregation**: +0.02 over pooling 12 slices at once.
